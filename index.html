Dark Matter & Dark Energy

The 95% of the universe we cannot see

Dhairya Kasundra [26324013], Gaurank Kumar [26324014], Kapu Aarya Teja [26324016], Kheni Shikha Ashvinbhai [26324017]

Problem Statement
  • Composition bar: Dark Matter (27%) | Dark Energy (68%) | Visible Matter (5%)
  • Visible matter is only ~5% of the universe; the other ~95% is dark and unidentified.
  • Dark matter: invisible mass that pulls on galaxies. Evidence: flat rotation curves, lensing, Bullet Cluster, CMB.
  • Dark energy: unknown driver of accelerating expansion (1998 supernovae, Nobel 2011).
  • Candidates: WIMPs, axions, primordial black holes; Λ or “quintessence”.
  • Key question: is dark energy constant, or does it change with time?
State of Technology
  • Direct detection: liquid-xenon detectors (LZ, XENONnT, PandaX-4T) and axion searches (ADMX). No signal yet.
  • DESI: 3D galaxy map hints (2-4σ) that dark energy may evolve.
  • Euclid & Rubin LSST: 2026 surveys mapping dark matter via lensing.
  • Status: research-stage; only spin-off hardware is commercial.

  • Environment: energy and xenon costs
  • Open science: data sharing and fair credit
  • Communication: 2-4σ “hints” are not discoveries
